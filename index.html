About Us

Our coffee bar uses only Ethiopian coffee. All commercially produced coffee originates from Ethiopia. We love Ethiopia for providing the world with coffee to grow, and we continue to love the flavor of that country's coffee. The fruity acidity of this single-origin coffee is balanced by its sweet honey notes, creating soft, light notes with a light character.

Our Ethiopian Heritage

At Ngopi Loer, our deep passion for exceptional coffee takes us directly to the misty highlands of Yirgacheffe and Sidamo in Ethiopia — the ancestral birthplace of Arabica coffee.

Every single batch is directly sourced from multi-generational farming cooperatives practicing shade-grown, organic harvesting methods. Our master roasters apply a precise artisanal profile to highlight delicate jasmine aromas, sparkling citrus acidity, and a velvety wildflower honey finish.
